Scenario & recording

Paste a level or generate a random one while Allan waits. Click Release to agent when you are done, then return to Agent view. Allan can begin only after release. Everything runs locally in this browser.

Legend: . floor, # solid wall, G glass wall (transparent, breakable), A Allan, C competitor, 1 Exit A, 2 Exit B, S stone, K knife, B brass key, I iron key, P bow, R rope, F food (apple), X bomb (can injure Allan). Paste 11 rows of 18 characters, or one compact 198-character string. Required: exactly one A (Allan) and at least one exit (1 or 2). All other symbols are optional, but each may occur at most once. With no iron key or bomb, exits start unlocked. Spaces and newlines are ignored.

Same-browser setup: paste a level, load it, release the agent, and switch back to Agent view. No server required.
Vision: unlimited across the entire map. Solid walls block sight; glass blocks movement but not sight. Leave both X and Y empty to omit an optional competitor or exit.
